ZumbaTech/Jobs/Interview

Schedule an Interview

Pick a time that works for you. We'll meet, talk shop, and get to know each other.

What to expect

Our process is designed to be friendly, transparent and respectful of your time. Three conversations, real engineering problems, and a chance to meet the team you'd actually work with.

01 Intro Chat

30 minutes with our hiring lead. We learn about your background and what you're looking for.

02 Technical

A pair-programming session with two engineers on a problem close to what we actually work on.

03 Team Fit

Meet your would-be teammates, your product manager, and ask us anything.
